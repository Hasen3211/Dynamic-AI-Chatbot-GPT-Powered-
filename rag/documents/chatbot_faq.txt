Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is this chatbot?
A: It is a GPT-powered conversational AI application capable of maintaining context and providing dynamic responses.

Q: Does the chatbot remember previous messages?
A: Yes. Conversation memory stores recent messages so the chatbot can maintain conversational context.

Q: What is RAG?
A: RAG stands for Retrieval-Augmented Generation. It retrieves relevant information from a knowledge base and provides that information to the language model before generating a response.

Q: Can the chatbot answer questions from documents?
A: Yes. The RAG layer searches the available knowledge documents and provides relevant information as context.

Q: Does the chatbot analyze sentiment?
A: Yes. The system includes a sentiment analysis module for identifying the general emotional tone of user messages.

Q: Does the chatbot identify user intent?
A: Yes. The intent detection module helps classify the purpose of a user message.

Q: Can the conversation be cleared?
A: Yes. Users can clear the current conversation memory.

Q: Is chat history stored?
A: Yes. Conversation exchanges can be stored in the application's chat history file.
